How it works

1

You create an automation

Choose a template below and fill in the form — who gets paid, and when.

2

You fund it whenever

Top up in any number of deposits. Every deposit is a normal Sepolia transaction.

3

Reactive watches for you

A contract on Reactive Lasna listens for your vault's deposit events and checks the total against your target — on-chain, in about a second.

4

It finishes itself

Target reached? A callback lands back on Sepolia and releases the funds to your beneficiary automatically. That's it — you're done since step 1.

Under the hood

For the curious (and the judges): every automation above is three small contracts and one event.

Sepolia                     Reactive Lasna                Sepolia
─────────                   ──────────────                ─────────
Vault.deposit() ──event──▶  GoalReactive.react() ──callback──▶ GoalCallback
  emits Deposited             checks total ≥ target             onGoalReached()
  (total + target             (one-shot, on-chain)                    │
   travel in the event)                                               ▼
                                                              Vault.release()
                                                              → beneficiary paid
